[h1]Headcount - Breeding Pod Population Projection[/h1]

How many beavers can your Iron Teeth breeding pods support?

[h2]Iron Teeth[/h2]

The [b]~N[/b] shows where your population could settle over time, based on how often your pods are running and how long your beavers live. It includes both standard and advanced breeding pods.

Hover over this for more details.

[h2]Folktails[/h2]

The [b]/N[/b] next to your population shows your bed count for the colony or selected district.

[h2]How the breeding pod estimate is calculated[/h2]

Steady state: population = birth rate × how long each beaver lives. 

- Births per pod come from the pod's [b]CycleLengthInDays × CyclesUntilFullyGrown[/b].
- Lifespan is the game's average lifespan, multiplied by the average [b]LifeExpectancy[/b] bonus of your living adults.

[h3]Interesting side notes[/h3]
- Kits grow up in `DaysOfChildhood ÷ GrowthSpeed`. Their life clock keeps running during childhood, so adults born from kits live for the remainder of the lifespan.
- Each beaver's has a random ±10% longevity.

Available for Iron Teeth (and Folktails)
Requires Timberborn 1.1.0 or later.
